Bariqly Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Bariqly is an Islamic companion app designed to help users with prayer times, Quran reading, Hadith, Duas, Qibla, Tasbeeh, Khatam tracking, Zakat calculations, Halal scanning and community Prayer Spots.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Bariqly processes, how it is used, and how you can contact us about your data.

1. Information We Process

Anonymous app identifier

Bariqly uses Firebase Anonymous Authentication to create an anonymous identifier. This identifier helps associate community submissions and bug reports with the user who submitted them. It does not require a conventional email-and-password account.

Location information

With your permission, Bariqly uses device location to calculate prayer times and support location-based features. You may also select a location manually instead of granting device location permission.

When you submit a Prayer Spot, its location coordinates may be stored in our database so other users can discover it.

Photos and camera

If you choose to use features that involve images, such as submitting a Prayer Spot or using the Halal scanner, Bariqly may access a photo you select or capture. Prayer Spot images are uploaded to Cloudinary, and their image URLs are stored with the associated community submission.

Community submissions and bug reports

Information you voluntarily submit may include Prayer Spot names, descriptions, amenities, photographs, location coordinates, confirmations, reports and bug report details or screenshots. These submissions are processed using Firebase services.

Analytics information

Bariqly uses Firebase Analytics to understand app usage and improve its features. Events include Khatam challenge activity, Khatam milestones, Halal scans, Prayer Spot submissions, bug report submissions and support purchases.

Information stored on your device

Quran bookmarks, reading position, Khatam progress, Tasbeeh progress and app preferences may be stored locally on your device. These are not intentionally transmitted as religious reading-progress records to our backend.

Purchases

Bariqly offers an in-app support feature. Payment transactions are handled through the applicable app store payment system. Bariqly does not ask you to send payment-card details directly to us.

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide prayer times and location-based features.
  • To display and manage community Prayer Spots.
  • To process user-submitted reports and feedback.
  • To maintain app functionality and preferences.
  • To understand feature usage and improve Bariqly.
  • To process voluntary support purchases.

3. Third-Party Services

Bariqly uses third-party services to operate certain features. These services process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable agreements.

  • Google Firebase: anonymous authentication, database services and app analytics. Firebase Privacy
  • Cloudinary: hosting images submitted for community features. Cloudinary Privacy
  • App Store payment services: processing in-app purchases through the relevant platform.

4. Data Sharing and Sale

We do not sell personal information. Information is processed by the service providers described above to operate Bariqly. Community submissions may be visible to other authenticated users, depending on the feature.

5. Data Retention

Local information may remain on your device until it is cleared or the app is uninstalled. Server-side information may remain in Firebase or Cloudinary until it is deleted through the applicable service or following a verified deletion request.

Specific retention periods for analytics and hosted images depend on the relevant service configuration. We do not currently publish a fixed retention period for all data.

6. Data Deletion Requests

You can request deletion of information associated with your Bariqly submissions by contacting us at:

bariqly.help@gmail.com

Include your anonymous Bariqly user ID, if available, and describe the information you want deleted. We may request additional information to verify the request.

Requests involving Firestore records and hosted images may require manual processing. We will review requests and explain any information that cannot be deleted.

7. Security

Bariqly uses third-party infrastructure and encrypted network connections for supported data transfers. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Children's Privacy

Bariqly is not knowingly designed to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws. If you believe a child has provided information improperly, contact us.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Bariqly's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.
