Bariqly Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Bariqly is an Islamic companion app designed to help users with prayer times, Quran reading, Hadith, Duas, Qibla, Tasbeeh, Khatam tracking, Zakat calculations, Halal scanning and community Prayer Spots.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Bariqly processes, why it is used, which services help process it and how you can contact us about your information.

1. Information We Process

Anonymous app identifier

Bariqly uses Firebase Anonymous Authentication to create an anonymous user identifier. This identifier helps associate community submissions and bug reports with the app installation that submitted them. It does not require you to register with an email address and password.

Location information

With your permission, Bariqly may access your device location to calculate prayer times and support location-based features. You can also choose a location manually instead of granting device location permission. You can manage or revoke location permission through your device settings.

When you submit a community Prayer Spot, its location coordinates are stored with the submission so that other users can discover the place.

Photos and camera

If you choose to use an image-related feature, Bariqly may access a photo you select or capture. When you submit a Prayer Spot with a photo, the image is uploaded to Cloudinary. The resulting image URL is stored with the associated community submission in Firebase.

Images selected for Halal scanning are processed on your device by the scanner's text-recognition workflow. The barcode lookup feature sends the scanned product barcode to Open Food Facts.

Community submissions and reports

If you use community features, information you submit may include Prayer Spot names, categories, descriptions, amenities, photos, location coordinates, confirmations and reports. Prayer Spot submissions are associated with your anonymous app identifier.

Prayer Spot information can be read by authenticated Bariqly users. Confirmation and report records are restricted by the current database rules to the authenticated user associated with each record.

Bug reports

If you submit a bug report, Bariqly may process the details and any screenshots you choose to provide. Bug reports are associated with your anonymous app identifier. Under the current database rules, a report can be read by its submitting user.

Analytics information

Bariqly uses Firebase Analytics to understand feature usage and improve the app. Events implemented in the app include Khatam challenge activity, Khatam milestones, Halal scans, Prayer Spot submissions, bug report submissions and support-purchase events.

Some analytics information may be collected automatically by Firebase and may include app usage, app instance identifiers, device or operating system information and approximate geographic information. The information collected can depend on the platform and service configuration.

Our configured Google Analytics retention settings are two months for event data and fourteen months for user-level data covered by those settings. These settings do not necessarily determine how long aggregated reports or other service records are retained.

Information stored on your device

Bariqly stores certain app information locally, which may include Quran bookmarks, reading position, Khatam progress, Tasbeeh progress and app preferences. These records are intended to remain on your device and are not intentionally uploaded to our backend as religious reading-progress records.

Local information may be affected by your device's backup, restore and application-storage settings.

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide prayer times and location-based features.
  • To display and manage community Prayer Spots.
  • To associate submissions, confirmations and reports with users.
  • To receive and investigate bug reports and feedback.
  • To maintain app functionality and preferences.
  • To understand feature usage and improve Bariqly.

3. Third-Party Services

Bariqly relies on third-party services for certain app features. Those providers process information under their own privacy policies and applicable terms.

  • Google Firebase: used for anonymous authentication, database services and analytics. Firebase Privacy
  • Cloudinary: used to host images submitted with community features. Cloudinary Privacy
  • Open Food Facts: used to look up product information using scanned barcodes. Open Food Facts Privacy

4. Data Sharing and Sale

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by the service providers described in this policy to operate Bariqly's features.

Prayer Spot submissions are available to authenticated users so they can discover community locations. Confirmation and report records are subject to the access restrictions described above. We may also disclose information where required by law or where necessary to protect the security and rights of users and the service.

5. Data Retention

Information stored locally may remain on your device until you clear the relevant app data or uninstall the app. Device backups may affect whether some local information remains available.

Community submissions, bug reports and associated database records may remain in Firebase until they are deleted through the applicable administrative process. Community records do not currently have a published automatic expiry period.

Images uploaded to Cloudinary may remain hosted until they are deleted through the applicable Cloudinary process. We do not currently publish a fixed automatic deletion period for hosted images.

Analytics retention is described in the Analytics section above. Actual retention may also depend on provider settings and applicable service policies.

6. Data Deletion Requests

You can request deletion of information associated with your Bariqly app identifier by contacting:

bariqly.help@gmail.com

To help us locate your records, open Bariqly and go to Settings → Help & Support → Copy Support ID. Include the copied Support ID in your email and describe the information you want deleted.

Deletion requests are handled manually. Depending on the request, this may involve separate deletion of Firebase database records, associated subcollection records, hosted Cloudinary images and the anonymous authentication identity. Deleting one item does not necessarily delete related information held by another service.

We will review requests and explain if any information cannot be deleted or must be retained for a legal or legitimate operational reason. We do not currently provide an automated in-app deletion process or promise a fixed completion timeframe.

7. Security

Bariqly uses third-party infrastructure and security controls intended to protect information. Network connections to supported services use encrypted connections where provided. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Children's Privacy

Bariqly does not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable privacy laws. If you believe a child has provided information inappropriately, please contact us so that we can review the matter.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Bariqly's features, service providers or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.

10. Contact Us

For privacy questions or data requests, contact:

bariqly.help@gmail.com
